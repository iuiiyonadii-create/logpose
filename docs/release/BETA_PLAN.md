# LogPose Beta Test Plan

## Objective
Identify real-world usability issues, bluetooth connectivity edge cases, and voice command reliability in high-noise environments.

## Target Audience
- Professional riders (delivery).
- Daily commuters.
- Long-distance touring riders.

## Phase 1: Internal Group (5 users)
- Validation of core background persistence.
- Basic music and call control.

## Phase 2: Expanded Group (20 users)
- Multi-app notification reading tests.
- Safety prioritization validation.

## Success Criteria
- 95% successful reconnection rate.
- 80% voice command accuracy at speeds up to 60km/h.
- Zero critical battery drain incidents (>20% per hour).
