# LogPose Known Issues & Limitations (MVP 1.0)

## Bluetooth
- **SCO Audio Latency**: Some older intercoms may experience a 1-2 second delay before voice responses start.
- **Auto-Pairing**: On some Android 14 devices, manual authorization is still required for the first connection.

## Voice Engine
- **Wind Noise**: At speeds above 80km/h, speech recognition accuracy drops significantly.
- **Accents**: Natural language processing is optimized for standard Spanish (ES); regional slang support is limited.

## Notifications
- **WhatsApp Calls**: Currently detected as standard notifications; voice "Answer" command may not work on all device models.

## Battery
- **Background Usage**: High microphone usage during active trips may increase battery consumption by ~5-8% per hour.
